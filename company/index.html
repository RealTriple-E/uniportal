Integrity block

What we don't claim.

Trust is built through precision, not adjectives. So here — in writing — is what this site does not assert.

  • No invented clients. We name no customer organisations, and no sector page implies one. Testimonials appear only with named consent.
  • No invented statistics. No customer counts, uptime percentages or adoption figures appear anywhere on this site.
  • No claimed certifications or partnerships. No ISO, SOC or partner logos. Capabilities are described as capabilities — mobile-money-ready payments, WhatsApp Business interface, no logos.
  • Concepts are labelled as concepts. AI, automation and intelligence pages carry their strategic-concept status openly.
How we describe status

LIVE — commercial products, published plans
STRATEGIC CONCEPT — approved direction, scoped per deployment
DEMONSTRATION DATA — sample figures, never live records
