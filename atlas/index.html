Four modes

Four views over one Intelligence State.

Not four databases — four perspectives on the same evidence and permission model.

What is happening now? — current events · geography · active alerts · resources · source health · operational status

Command keeps the current picture in one place: what is active, where it sits, which sources are fresh, and what needs attention first. Synthetic data

How is this information connected? — entities · relationships · cases · evidence · timelines · cross-case correlation

Fusion assembles the case: who and what is involved, how items relate, what the evidence supports, and what remains unverified. Identity matches stay as candidates until an authorised person confirms them.

What are we doing about it? — incidents · resources · tasks · acknowledgements · coordination · outcomes

Response coordinates the work: tasks move through a controlled state machine — created, assigned, acknowledged, in progress, completed, verified — with every transition attributed and timestamped.

What is changing and what requires leadership attention? — trends · baselines · change · priorities · performance · briefings

Strategic compares current state against baselines, surfaces what changed, and packages governed briefings with probability and confidence kept separate.

Evidence & provenance

Every claim answers: why should I believe this?

Assessments trace back through evidence to source records — with conflicts preserved, not overwritten.

Who · When · Transformations · Corroboration · Limitations

Observed, calculated, correlated, assessed, simulated and forecast material is never silently mixed.

  • Provenance on everything. Source, collection method, timestamp, licence and transformation history.
  • Conflicts preserved. Disagreeing sources are shown side by side with reliability and review status.
  • Minimum disclosure. Cross-case matches trigger a permission check before anything is shared.

Where Atlas could help

Representative uses, in conditional language.

What Atlas could be configured for — not deployments claimed to already run.

Use 01

Operations oversight

An operations desk could watch events, resources and source health in Command — and brief leadership from the same state.

Use 02

Investigation support

An investigation team could assemble entities, timelines and evidence in Fusion — with inference labelled as inference.

Use 03

Response coordination

A coordination cell could assign, acknowledge and verify tasks in Response — and review outcomes afterwards.

Use 04

Executive briefing

Leadership could receive governed briefings in Strategic — with sources, limitations and change from baseline attached.
