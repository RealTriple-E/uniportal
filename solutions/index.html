Our labelling promise

What "representative workflow" means.

Most vendors show sector pages as finished deployments. We show them as what they are: configuration patterns we can build for you.

  • Use case, not user story. Flows describe the shape of a solution, not a client we have served.
  • "Can be configured for" language throughout. Capability is stated; deployment is scoped with you.
  • Demonstrations are labelled. Anything shown with sample figures carries a demonstration-data tag.
