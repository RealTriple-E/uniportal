What organisations ask before they commit.

Every answer below reflects the current, verifiable position. Where scope varies, we say so.

In Zambian Kwacha. Institutional products carry published monthly plans — University / College Management from K2,100, HR & Payroll from K1,299, and the Institutional Combo from K2,299. See the pricing page for full plan tables.

Each plan table lists its modules — records, payments, communication, analytics — plus its support tier. Anything outside the table is scoped separately and quoted in writing. Start at pricing, then confirm on a demo.

Enquiry, demonstration, scoping, configuration, migration where supported and agreed, staff training, then go-live with plan support. Timelines depend on your data readiness. See services for the delivery ladder.

Where supported and agreed, yes — spreadsheets, exports and existing databases move across during implementation. What can and cannot migrate is confirmed at scoping, before you commit, and recorded in your quote.

Support and maintenance follow your plan tier, as listed in each plan table — no invented SLAs, no vague "priority support". Enterprise and AI engagements carry their own support terms, agreed in writing per engagement.

WhatsApp or call +260 973 981 779, or email uniportal.hq@gmail.com. The demo form and contact page both hand off to WhatsApp and email, so nothing submits into a void.

You describe how your organisation runs; we show the matching product on realistic demonstration data — clearly labelled, never your live records and never presented as a client deployment. Institutional demos can lead straight to a scoped quote.

Identity, role and purpose-based access, customer isolation, provenance on records, audit trails and human approval for consequential actions are designed into the platform. Exact controls depend on deployment — we document what is real and claim no certifications. See security.

UniPortal AI is a strategic concept: an approved direction with an internal product plan, scoped per deployment — not an off-the-shelf subscription. Talk to us and we will scope what it can be configured for in your organisation. See UniPortal AI.
