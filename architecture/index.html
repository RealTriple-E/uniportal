How to read the stack

Bottom-up for builders. Top-down for leaders.

Technical readers follow data upward; executives follow decisions downward. Both meet at governance.

  • Upward: sources become records, records become state, state becomes answers and action.
  • Downward: every decision traces back through evidence to its approved source.
  • Across: governance and security span all layers rather than sitting at the edge.
