Why gates matter

Automation that knows when to stop.

Low-risk steps may run directly; high-risk actions — payments, external messages, distribution — are designed to wait.

  • Named approvers. Each gate names who may approve, and records the decision.
  • Full context. Approvers see the draft, the source and the reasoning first.
  • Measured after. Outcomes feed reporting, so the workflow improves.
