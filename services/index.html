Delivery ladder

Climb one rung at a time.

Each rung is agreed before the next begins, so scope, cost and timing stay visible.

  1. Rung 01

    Foundation — records and roles

    Core records, users and permissions set up and checked with your team.

  2. Rung 02

    Connection — sources and systems

    Approved sources linked; integrations scoped, connected and tested.

  3. Rung 03

    Intelligence — answers and briefings

    Assistants, reports and alerts configured within role boundaries.

  4. Rung 04

    Operation — workflows and support

    Automations go live with approval gates; support takes over after go-live.
