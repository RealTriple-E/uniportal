Permission model

Four levels. Approval where it matters.

What the assistant may do is agreed per role — and anything consequential waits for a person.

Read

Answer & summarise

Read approved records and answer with sources. The default level for most roles.

Write

Draft & prepare

Prepare reports, messages and records as drafts — never sent or filed without approval.

Execute

Run agreed steps

Carry out low-risk agreed steps directly, fully logged and reversible where possible.

Approval-required

Draft action prepared → Queued with context → Named approver decides → Executed + logged

Payments, external messages, distribution and other high-risk actions are designed to need human approval first.

WhatsApp-first rationale

Meet staff where they already are.

Many Zambian teams run on WhatsApp. UniPortal AI is designed to answer, brief and request approvals there — with the same permissions as web.

  • No training burden. Staff ask and approve in a familiar channel.
  • Same control. Role, purpose and approval rules apply on every channel.
  • Enterprise links. Customer accounting, CRM and HR systems can connect where appropriate.
