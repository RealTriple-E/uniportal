In the working MVP

The chair, the book and the till.

Six areas that work today for salons and barbershops.

01

Appointments

A simplified appointment workflow — booked clients placed against the day, without a second system.

02

Walk-ins

Walk-ins handled the same way as booked clients, so the front desk never loses the thread.

03

Service menu

Services registered with names and prices — the menu that gets sold at the chair.

04

Customer records

Regular clients kept with their visit history — recognisable when they return.

05

Payment at the chair

Cash, mobile money, card or split, with change calculated — completed where the service happened.

06

Who served whom

Users, roles and work sessions attach every service and payment to the person who did it.

Honest scope

What works today, and what is planned.

Kora's MVP ships the daily flow below. The second column is roadmap — planned, not shipped.

Working MVP

Available today

  • Simplified appointment and walk-in workflow
  • Services registered with prices
  • Customer records with visit history
  • Payments including split payment and automatic change
  • Users, roles and work sessions
  • Offline operation with automatic synchronisation
Roadmap

Planned for the full platform

  • Sophisticated commission rules
  • Loyalty programmes
  • Advanced CRM and communication history
  • Multi-branch operations
  • WhatsApp-based booking

No published Kora prices — every business is scoped by type, size and devices, and the demonstration is temporary while the product is in early use. Talk to us for a written scope.
