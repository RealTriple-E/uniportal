In the working MVP

The bay, on one screen.

Six areas that work today for car wash businesses.

01

Queue that moves

Vehicles enter a queue and move through it — visible to everyone working the bay.

02

Services & prices

Wash packages and add-ons registered as services with prices, sold in a tap.

03

Payment at the gate

Cash, mobile money, card or split — change calculated, receipt shared or printed.

04

Customer records

Repeat customers kept with their visit history — useful for returning vehicles.

05

Stock for what you sell

Soap, polish and air fresheners reduce stock like any other product.

06

Shift totals

Each work session opens with counted cash and closes with totals against the queue served.

Honest scope

What works today, and what is planned.

Kora's MVP ships the daily flow below. The second column is roadmap — planned, not shipped.

Working MVP

Available today

  • Vehicle → service → queue → completion → payment flow
  • Services registered with prices
  • Payments including split payment and automatic change
  • Customers and stock for retail items
  • Users, roles and work sessions with closing totals
  • Offline operation with automatic synchronisation
Roadmap

Planned for the full platform

  • Online reservations and pre-booking
  • Loyalty programmes
  • Multi-branch operations
  • Advanced CRM and communication history
  • Route or field-service extensions

No published Kora prices — every business is scoped by type, size and devices, and the demonstration is temporary while the product is in early use. Talk to us for a written scope.
