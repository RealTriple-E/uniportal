How they fit

Start where the need is greatest. Connect the rest later.

An institution can begin with payroll, a college with student records — and grow into a connected operating environment without replacing what works.

  • Live products first. Institutional plans are published in Kwacha with clear scopes.
  • AI where it earns trust. Assistants and automations connect to approved sources only.
  • Intelligence when you are ready. Athena and Atlas govern the picture across systems.
