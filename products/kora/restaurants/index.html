In the working MVP

Service, settled and recorded.

Six areas that work today for restaurants and bars.

01

Table, order, bill

A simplified flow from table to order to bill — built for a phone or tablet at the pass.

02

Menu as products

Dishes and drinks registered as products with prices, so every plate and pour is accounted for.

03

Payments, including splits

Cash, mobile money, card, bank or a split between them — change calculated automatically.

04

Quick sale at the bar

Fast-entry selling for counter and bar service, without opening a table.

05

Basic tabs

Basic tab handling for open rounds; advanced tab controls are roadmap.

06

Stock follows the kitchen

Ingredients and drinks sold reduce stock, with counts and visible discrepancies.

Honest scope

What works today, and what is planned.

Kora's MVP ships the daily flow below. The second column is roadmap — planned, not shipped.

Working MVP

Available today

  • Simplified table / order / bill flow
  • Menu items as products with prices
  • Split payments and automatic change
  • Quick sale and basic tabs for the bar
  • Stock deductions, stocktake and low-stock signals
  • Offline operation with automatic synchronisation
Roadmap

Planned for the full platform

  • Full restaurant kitchen management
  • Advanced bar tabs and happy-hour pricing rules
  • Reservations
  • Delivery management
  • Multi-branch operations

No published Kora prices — every business is scoped by type, size and devices, and the demonstration is temporary while the product is in early use. Talk to us for a written scope.
