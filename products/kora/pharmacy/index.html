Pharmacy — Kora by UniPortal

Batch and expiry on the products that need them.

For pharmacies and dispensaries — a dispensing counter where every product can carry batch and expiry information, and every sale names the person who made it.

In the working MVP

The counter, covered honestly.

Six areas that work today — with specialised regulatory workflows explicitly not claimed.

01

Dispensing till

Search or scan, take payment in cash, mobile money, card or split, with automatic change and a printed or shared receipt.

02

Batch & expiry fields

Products that need them carry batch and expiry details in the record, visible to the person dispensing.

03

Stock & stocktake

Stock moves with every sale; counts can be taken and any difference between expected and counted stays visible.

04

Customer records

Customer purchase history kept with the transaction — retrievable when a question comes back later.

05

Credit recorded

Credit sales recorded with what is owed, instead of living only in a notebook.

06

Who dispensed it

Users, roles and work sessions attach every transaction to the staff member and shift responsible.

Honest scope

What works today, and what is planned.

Kora's MVP ships the daily flow below. The second column is roadmap — planned, not shipped.

Working MVP

Available today

  • Batch and expiry fields on product records
  • Till with scanning, split payments and automatic change
  • Inventory, stocktake and visible discrepancies
  • Customer records and recorded credit sales
  • Audit trail of user, session and device per transaction
  • Offline operation with automatic synchronisation
Roadmap

Planned for the full platform

  • Sophisticated pharmacy workflows (explicitly deferred)
  • Regulatory or statutory reporting of any kind
  • Loyalty and advanced CRM
  • Multi-branch operations and warehouses
  • Supplier management beyond product linkage

No published Kora prices — every business is scoped by type, size and devices, and the demonstration is temporary while the product is in early use. Talk to us for a written scope. Kora makes no regulatory or certification claims.
