Representative workflow

From field records to funder confidence.

Existing plans and sheets stay where they are. UniPortal connects to approved copies and builds the operating picture.

  • Projects, budgets and documents connect as sources. Work plans, budget trackers and document folders — scoped per deployment.
  • Every figure keeps its provenance. A reported number traces to the record it came from — essential when a donor asks "show me".
  • Access follows roles. Field teams, finance and leadership each see what their role allows — including over WhatsApp.
