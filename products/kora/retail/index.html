In the working MVP

The shop floor, covered end to end.

Six areas that work today for retail and grocery businesses.

01

Till that keeps up

Search or scan with the device camera, build the cart and take payment — cash, mobile money, card, bank, credit or a split, with change calculated.

02

Stock that follows every sale

Quantities reduce as sales complete — no end-of-day reconciliation to discover surprises.

03

Low-stock alerts

Reorder points on your products, with low and out-of-stock signals on the dashboard.

04

Sales history & returns

Find any transaction by date, user or product; record returns and refunds against the original sale.

05

Customers & credit

Regular customers kept with their purchase history; credit sales recorded with the balance owed.

06

Shifts & accountability

Every session opens with counted cash and closes with totals — every sale names the cashier who made it.

Honest scope

What works today, and what is planned.

Kora's MVP ships the daily flow below. The second column is roadmap — planned, not shipped.

Working MVP

Available today

  • Barcode scanning (device camera) and product search at the till
  • Split payments across at least two methods, automatic change
  • Stock deductions, stocktake and visible discrepancies
  • Sales history, returns and refunds
  • Users, roles, work sessions and an audit trail
  • Offline selling with automatic synchronisation
Roadmap

Planned for the full platform

  • Multi-branch operations and warehouses
  • Loyalty programmes and advanced CRM
  • Delivery management
  • Automated purchase recommendations from suppliers
  • Advanced stock valuation and shrinkage analytics

No published Kora prices — every business is scoped by type, size and devices, and the demonstration is temporary while the product is in early use. Talk to us for a written scope.
