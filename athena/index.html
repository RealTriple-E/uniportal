How Athena is built

Three cores. One Intelligence State.

Athena connects approved systems and preserves source meaning while making relationships and change understandable.

  • Data Foundation. Connectors, source registry, geography, quality and search over authorised records.
  • Intelligence Core. Ontology, entities, relationships, evidence, baselines, change and assessment.
  • Governance Core. Identity, policy, tenancy, audit, provenance and retention — architectural, not add-ons.

Human control

AI assists. Humans decide.

AI explains, summarises and suggests against the evidence base. Sensitive actions wait for explicit approval.

  • Evidence contract. Substantive answers carry sources, time range, scope, limitations — and both probability and confidence.
  • Approval gates. High-impact steps are drafted, reviewed and approved — never silently executed.
  • Versioned history. Corrections create new governed versions; history is never silently rewritten.
Probability ≠ confidence

Probability 0.72 — how likely the outcome is assessed to be
Confidence 0.88 — how confident the assessment is
These values are never collapsed into one score.

Demonstration vs production is always visually labelled: demonstration uses synthetic or public data; pilot uses approved real data; production uses governed operational data.
