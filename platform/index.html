How the layers fit

One canvas, three views of the same organisation.

The layers share people, records and permissions — so a student, a payslip or a sales figure is named once and reused everywhere.

  • Each layer works alone. Adopt institutional systems, AI or intelligence in any order.
  • Records are shared. Entities resolve once, so reports agree with source systems.
  • Control is consistent. The same identity and permission model applies across layers.

Integration model

Connect what exists. Keep working the way you work.

UniPortal is designed to sit above approved sources — spreadsheets, accounting packages, databases, documents and APIs — and make them usable.

  • Connectors link approved systems and files, with access agreed per source.
  • APIs allow customer systems to exchange records with the platform where appropriate.
  • Permission-first. Every connection respects the same role and purpose model.

Deployment options

Hosted your way, where appropriate.

Deployment is agreed per customer — the same platform, delivered under the arrangement that fits.

Option A

Customer-hosted

Infrastructure under the customer's control, with UniPortal configured and supported on it — where appropriate.

Option B

UniPortal-managed

UniPortal operates the environment on the customer's behalf, with agreed access and handling.

Option C

Hybrid

Sensitive records stay with the customer while shared services run where agreed — scoped per deployment.
