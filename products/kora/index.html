What Kora is

Complexity underneath. Simplicity on top.

One platform with a common operational engine — not a separate app for every industry.

The idea

Start where the business is today

Kora is built for businesses that may currently run on notebooks, calculators, WhatsApp or Excel — and for the multi-branch business they may become. Business type is only a starting point; the capabilities a business actually needs are switched on around it.

The model

Templates over one engine

A grocery starts with retail, inventory and credit. A salon starts with appointments, services and commissions. Behind both sits the same products, sales, stock, customer and audit records — so moving up never means starting again.

  • One record per event. Sales, returns, stock movements and expenses are recorded once and stay traceable.
  • Offline-first. The till keeps selling without internet and synchronises automatically when connectivity returns.
  • Everyone accountable. Every transaction carries the user and work session that performed it.

In the working MVP

What Kora does today.

Eight areas that already work end to end — from registering a product to reviewing the day's takings.

01

Point of Sale

Search or scan a barcode, build the cart, take cash, mobile money, card, bank transfer, credit or a split — with change calculated automatically.

02

Products & Import

Names, barcodes, prices, categories and stock levels — registered one by one or brought in from CSV/Excel in bulk.

03

Inventory & Stocktake

Stock reduces on every sale, counts can be taken quickly or in full, and every discrepancy between expected and counted stays visible.

04

Sales History & Returns

Every transaction retrievable by date, user or product — with returns and refunds recorded against the original sale.

05

Customers & Credit

Customer records with purchase history; credit sales recorded with the balance owed, so nothing lives only in a notebook.

06

Users, Roles & Shifts

Cashiers, managers and owners see different screens. Each work session opens, records and closes with its own totals.

07

Offline-First Sync

Entries save locally first — no waiting on a server, no manual Save button — and synchronise when the connection returns.

08

Manager & Owner Dashboards

Sales today, transaction count, payment mix, low stock and inventory value — on a phone, tablet or desktop.

Honest scope

Working now, and planned next.

Working MVP

Available in the product today

  • POS with barcode scanning, split payments and automatic change
  • Product registration and bulk CSV/Excel import
  • Inventory, stocktake and sales history with returns
  • Customers, recorded credit sales and expenses
  • Users, roles, work sessions and an audit trail
  • Offline operation with automatic synchronisation

Roadmap

  • Kora Control operations console and release tooling
