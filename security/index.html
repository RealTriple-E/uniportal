Control families

Twelve families, in careful language.

Each control is described as design intent. Confirm exact behaviour for your deployment before relying on it.

01

Access Control

Named user accounts with least-privilege defaults. Access is granted by role and reviewed with the customer.

02

MFA Where Supported

Multi-factor authentication is designed to be used where the deployment and channel support it.

03

RBAC

Role-based access control: permissions follow job function, so a change of role changes what is visible.

04

Purpose-Based Permissions

Beyond role, access can be limited by purpose — the same record may answer one question but not another.

05

Customer Isolation

Each customer's records are kept separate by design, so one organisation never sees another's data.

06

Audit Logs

Access, answers and actions are designed to be recorded with who, what and when for later review.

07

Provenance

Figures carry their source — what system they came from, when, and under what authority.

08

Encryption

Encryption in transit is designed in; encryption at rest applies where applicable per deployment.

09

Backups & Retention

Backup and retention handling are agreed per deployment, with restore and disposal paths documented.

10

Human Approval

Consequential and high-risk actions are drafted first and wait for a named person's approval.

11

Model & Provider Controls

Models work through a gateway with permission checks and logging — never directly on raw sources.

12

Deployment Models

Customer-hosted, UniPortal-managed or hybrid — where appropriate and agreed per customer.

Approvals and deployment

People decide. The platform records.

Automation recommends and drafts; named people approve. Deployment determines where records live and who operates them.

  • Approval gates sit before high-impact steps such as distribution, payment or external messages.
  • Attribution names the approver and the time, kept in the audit trail.
  • Deployment choice — customer-hosted, UniPortal-managed or hybrid — is agreed per customer.
