The SME story

The numbers already exist. They are just scattered.

A typical growing business keeps sales in one book, stock in another, and staff matters in memory. Business AI can be configured to join them.

Use case 01

Morning briefing

Yesterday's sales, today's stock risks and anything needing your attention — delivered each morning, on WhatsApp or email. Can be configured for your reporting rhythm.

Use case 02

Ask anything

"Which product moved fastest this month?" "Who owes us, and how much?" Answers come with their source, limited to what your role may see.

Use case 03

Act with approval

Reorder drafts, reminder messages and staff notices are prepared for you. Consequential steps wait for your approval — never silently executed.

WhatsApp-first

No new app to learn. No computer required.

Your team already uses WhatsApp. Business AI can be configured to meet them there — with the same permissions as the web view.

  • Role-aware answers. A shop assistant and the owner ask the same question and correctly receive different levels of detail.
  • Your systems stay. Spreadsheets, books and existing software connect as approved sources — nothing is ripped out.
  • Scoped pricing, in writing. Business AI is scoped per business, so there is no fixed price list — talk to UniPortal for a written quote.
