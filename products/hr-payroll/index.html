Capabilities

From employee file to payday, without spreadsheets.

Seven areas that cover staff administration and the monthly payroll run.

01

Employee Administration

Complete staff records — contracts, roles, banking details and documents — kept in one organised file.

02

Automatic Payslips

Salaries calculated from attendance, leave and allowances, with payslips generated for every run.

03

Statutory Files

NAPSA, NHIMA and ZRA contribution files prepared from payroll data for filing.

04

Leave Management

Leave requests, balances and approvals tracked against each employee's entitlement.

05

WhatsApp Self-Service

Staff check payslips, balances and leave over WhatsApp — fewer queries for the HR office.

06

Departmental Payroll

Payroll organised by department or branch, so costs are visible where they belong.

07

Cloud Reports

Payroll and headcount reports available in the cloud for management review and audit.

08

Access Control

Role-based access so payroll details are visible only to the roles your policy allows.
