Why the Combo

Institutions run on two workforces: students and staff.

Most institutions buy these systems separately and reconcile them by hand. The Combo keeps both sides in one place.

Registration + Academics + Payments + Communication → University Management → results, income visibility, records

Full university and college coverage — see the product in detail.

Staff records + Attendance + Leave → HR & Payroll → payslips, statutory files, self-service

Full staff and payroll coverage — see the product in detail.

Students + Finance + Staff → One institutional view → leadership analytics, shared records, one subscription

Leadership sees the whole institution — people, money and performance — without reconciling two systems.

What the Combo adds

More than the sum of two subscriptions.

The Combo carries both product lines forward and adds the shared layer that connects them.

  • Core university tools — registration, records, results, payments and communication.
  • Salary calculation and statutory reports — payslips plus NAPSA / NHIMA / ZRA files.
  • Smart payroll and analytics on higher plans — performance, attendance and cloud-backed insight.
