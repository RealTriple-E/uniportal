Analyst learning paths

From orientation to assessed competence.

Paths progress in difficulty, and every step is practised on synthetic data — never on live systems.

  1. Stage 01

    Orient

    Learn the four Atlas views, the evidence chain, and why probability differs from confidence.

  2. Stage 02

    Practise

    Work guided scenarios with coaching: correlate, assess, propose actions, request approval.

  3. Stage 03

    Exercise

    Run full unguided scenarios under time pressure, including degraded sources and conflicts.

  4. Stage 04

    Evaluate

    Assessed exercises with after-action review — strengths, gaps and next training recorded.

Trainer vs operational Atlas

TRAINER — synthetic scenarios · practice tasks · learning records
≠
OPERATIONAL ATLAS — authorised data · real tasks · operational records
No shared data. No shared access. No confusion.

Hard boundary

A training environment must never be confused with live systems.

Trainer uses its own synthetic worlds, its own accounts and its own records — visually distinct at all times.

New analysts begin in Trainer long before they ever see a governed operational partition. Exercises, coaching notes and evaluation records live only inside the training environment, so practice work can never leak into operational cases, tasks or briefings.

  • Separate data. Only synthetic training scenarios — nothing operational, nothing live.

Trainer accounts cannot see operational partitions, and operational accounts never train inside live data.

  • Distinct labelling. Training tags and banners mark every screen, export and briefing.
  • Safe to fail. Mistakes become lessons in review — with no operational consequence.
