Governing principles

Restraint is the feature.

Public institutions carry duties a vendor must respect. This page states the boundaries before the capabilities.

Principle 01

Provenance before picture

Every figure traces to its authorised source and licence. Inferences are labelled as inferences — never mixed silently with observed fact.

Principle 02

Permissions before access

Identity, role and purpose gate every view. Customer data is isolated per tenant, and access is logged for audit.

Principle 03

Approvals before action

Consequential steps route through explicit human approval. The system recommends and drafts; people decide and are accountable.

What this can be configured for

Careful configurations, scoped in writing.

Each engagement starts from authorisation: which records, under what licence, for which named purpose.

  • Operational dashboards over authorised records. What is happening now, drawn only from sources the institution approves.
  • Synthetic demonstrations first. Concepts are shown on synthetic data — labelled as such — before any live record is connected. See Synthetic Data Studio.
  • Training without live data. Staff practise in Atlas Trainer, a separate environment that never touches operational records.
